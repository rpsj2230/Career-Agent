Filter Auto Apply

  1. LinkedIn: open Jobs, search, switch on Easy Apply and any other filters. Naukri: open a job list with your filters on.
  2. Open this popup on that tab, choose how many jobs, press Apply. Keep the tab open.
What it does and when it stops

For each job it skips what LinkedIn shows as Applied or that applies on the company's own site, asks Career Agent whether it may be sent, fills every step, presses Next and Submit, waits for the confirmation, and records it. A question it cannot answer is given back to you in Career Agent with the reason. It stops at a security check, a portal limit, a sign-in wall, or when Career Agent's limits are reached. It needs Auto-Apply on, and for LinkedIn also "Allow automated LinkedIn Easy Apply" in Career Agent Settings.

Automatic mode

Fills and sends the applications the assistant could not finish, one after another.

What it does and when it stops

It opens each application, fills it, attaches its resume, presses Next and Submit, waits for the employer's confirmation, then moves on. It gives one back to you, with the reason, at a captcha, a password, a question it cannot answer or a page it does not trust. It runs only when Auto-Apply is on in Career Agent and the daily limit is not used up.
